# OMOPHub Medical Terminology for ChatGPT

Look up medical terms and codes for diagnoses, lab tests and medications. Search by name or code, check a standard mapping when one exists, and explore broader or more specific terms.

## Connect an account

When the public listing is available, open **OMOPHub Medical Terminology** in the ChatGPT plugin directory and choose **Connect**. Sign in to OMOPHub and approve vocabulary access. The plugin uses the vocabularies and monthly read allowance of the account you connect. A mapping or paginated lookup can use more than one read.

In a chat with the plugin enabled, try “What lab test does LOINC 718-7 identify?” Provide just the vocabulary term or code. Keep patient and chart information out of your prompts.

OMOPHub supports read-only terminology research. It cannot change a vocabulary or recommend a treatment. To revoke access, open Settings → Connected Apps in the OMOPHub dashboard and disconnect ChatGPT.
